# CLIMS 2.6.0

- People manage their own profile and password. `PATCH /auth/me` changes name, email and phone (a new email or phone needs the current password), and `POST /auth/password` changes the password and signs out the account's other devices. Web: **Your profile** in the account menu. Field app: **Edit details** and **Change password** on Me.
- Accounts an administrator or district focal person creates must choose their own password at first sign-in. Administrators (`PUT /users/:id/password`) and DCFPs, for their own field workers (`PUT /users/district/:id/password`), can set a temporary password, which signs the account out everywhere and must be replaced at the next sign-in.
- Adds consumption reports by hotspot, district and warehouse (`GET /reports/consumption`), read from requests and what was delivered against them. Web: **Reports → Consumption** in the MOH, DCFP and warehouse workspaces, with CSV and Excel downloads. It replaces the monthly report screens.
- Removes report approval. Every report is a live calculation, downloaded as it stands. The approved versions and corrections endpoints, and the Approved versions tab, are gone.
- Hotspot pages show a map of the hotspot's location with the hotspots within a kilometre, and flag any within 100 m as a possible duplicate. `GET /hotspots` takes `near_lat`, `near_lng` and `radius_m`.
- MOH oversight on real data: deliveries to hotspots and NMS/JMS shipments to district stores on Distribution, a national hotspot list searched on the server, and CSV and Excel downloads on every Analytics tab. MOH's Reports and Procurement pages are removed.

## Upgrade from 2.5.0

Back up PostgreSQL before deploying. The backend applies three migrations at startup:

- 000042 adds `users.must_change_password` and `password_changed_at`. Existing accounts keep their passwords and are not asked to change them.
- 000043 drops the `report_versions` table and the `reports:approve` permission. **Any saved approved report versions are deleted.** Export them from the backup first if they are needed.
- 000044 adds an index on hotspot latitude and longitude.

There are no new production settings. Field workers need the 2.6.0 app. Field roles cannot use the web, so a new or temporary-password field account cannot get past its first sign-in on an older app.
